Compound Clips

Compound Clips is a personal finance short-video channel. This page describes the small private tool the channel owner uses to publish their own videos.

What the app does

The app runs on the channel owner's own computer. It cuts the owner's long videos into short vertical clips and uploads or schedules those clips on the owner's own YouTube channel and Facebook Page. It only connects to the accounts of its owner; it is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Read how data is handled in the privacy policy.
